Overview / Strategies
Strategy design · hypotheses, not claims

Strategy lab

A signal earns a place on the leaderboard by surviving prospective, out-of-sample tests after transaction costs—not by a hot streak. There are no measured strategy results yet.

Luck cannot be removed from individual game outcomes. We can reduce overconfidence by measuring uncertainty, controlling selection bias, testing on untouched future periods, and rewarding repeatable forecasting skill rather than raw short-run profit. No gate below is passed today.

How to originate a fair line (proposed model contract)

For each scheduled game, define home margin M = home points − away points and total T = home points + away points. Fit and calibrate a predictive joint distribution for (M,T) from only pre-cutoff, point-in-time features. Publish fair estimates as distributions, not a single unsupported point guess. No fit has been run because the repository has no game-level training data.
