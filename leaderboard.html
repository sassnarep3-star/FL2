Strategy leaderboard & rewards

Decision this page serves: how competing strategies are ranked and paid so that the winner is the one with skill rather than the one with variance. The board is empty by design; the rules for filling it are complete and implemented in engine/gates.py.

Design principles (each one removes a specific way to fake skill)

Pre-commitment before evaluation

A spec is hashed into the registry before its window opens, so “we tried 40 things and show you the best” becomes measurable rather than deniable — and the deflation term (G-04) has an honest N.

Rank on gates, then on edge, then on P&L

PnL is last and weighted by log of sample size. A 200-bet strategy that clears 16 gates outranks a 40-bet one with a bigger number, because only the first is real.

CLV is the primary skill statistic

Beating the close is what the market says about your information (G-03). Won-loss is an outcome of luck and sample size, so it is reported but never ranked on.

One driver, one bet

Correlation-cluster ids on every ticket (G-16); a five-leg SGP built from one injury is scored as one leveraged bet, not five independent ones.

Decay is a live requirement, not a post-mortem

G-08 benches strategies whose recent edge falls below the 10th percentile of their own history, and a promotion must survive two consecutive windows (T3).

Cap the upside that invites gaming

Rewards are bankroll allocation, not cash, and violation of a gate after the fact forfeits the window and re-publishes the spec — because the exploitable object in a competition is the scoring rule.

Competition configuration

Why the standing table is empty today

Filling the board requires four inputs none of which exist yet, and each is a numbered blocker rather than a to-do:

  • graded bets — needs a market to price against, then outcomes; blocked by FL-014 / Q-10
  • closing prices — the CLV column cannot be “0”, it must be absent; same blocker
  • a frozen baseline v0 — without a fixed reference, every “edge” is relative to a moving target (SG-13, Q-08)
  • measured ρ for combo legs — until the correlation register has values, any parlay rank would be priced on an assumption (Q-12)

What can be ranked right now, and is

The method is already competitive: 22 signals, each with declared inputs, expected direction, decay behaviour, correlation partners and the gates it must clear (signal catalogue). That ranking — “which hypothesis is worth building first” — is the one this repo can honestly produce today, and it is decided by candidate versus blocked status, not by enthusiasm:

  • Buildable now: SIG-06 (rest exclusion), SIG-22 (veto), SIG-03 (backup-QB flag), SIG-07 (trajectory divergence) — all computable from the official feeds this snapshot already reads
  • Buildable once the roster capture is complete: SIG-02, SIG-05
  • Buildable once market snapshots exist: SIG-09, SIG-11, SIG-13, SIG-18, SIG-19, SIG-21
  • Buildable once licensed usage data exists: SIG-01 share term, SIG-04, SIG-16, SIG-17

Operating notes for the competition

RuleDetail
Entry formatA JSON spec: id, family, market, feature list, thresholds, sizing rule, and the “what would change my mind” line (SG-02). Registered in data/strategy-registry.jsonl via engine/registry.py.
ReproducibilityEvery published figure must be regenerable by python3 -m engine.selftest-style command plus a named data snapshot (R-40). Numbers that cannot be regenerated are not on the board.
Missing evidenceRenders as unknown and blocks promotion. It never renders as a pass and never as a zero (the same rule the tables use for blank cells).
Sample floorG-01: 150 graded bets, or 4 seasons of games for a pure origination model. At a realistic 30 qualifying bets a week that is ≈5 weeks of evidence minimum — computed, not vibe-based.
Re-ratingEvery promotion is provisional: G-08 (decay), G-15 (revision sensitivity) and G-03 (CLV) are recomputed continuously, and a strategy is benched automatically on failure rather than argued about.
Anti-copyingRegistration timestamp + spec hash means a copied strategy cannot claim an earlier window; a copy discovered later forfeits its own rewards.
What the top reward buysFirst call on paid data (Q-01, Q-10, Q-19). The competition therefore funds the things that make the next competition measurable — which is the only self-improving loop available here.
