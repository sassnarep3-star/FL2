Flagged for review

What this page is for: everything that looked wrong, contradicted itself, or could not be confirmed against an official source — published instead of quietly fixed. Nothing here blocks the site or the merge; each row is a request for your judgement, with the source link and the single action that would close it.

Severity key

blocker-for-model-trust

A model built today would be wrong in a way that is not visible from the output. Fix before any gate is scored. One item: the absence of any market-data source.

high

Changes a feature definition, a join, or a legal posture. Safe to keep working around for a week, not for a season.

medium

A data-quality or interpretation issue that will quietly bias results if it is never resolved.

low

Noted for completeness and for the crawler spec; no current decision depends on it.

counting rule

items: high-or-blocking. Registers themselves carry questions and suggestions — those are decisions to make, not defects, and are kept on their own pages.

disposition

Every item stays open until a human or a future session records a resolution in COORDINATION.md. Closing an item means editing its row with the source that settled it, not deleting it.

All flagged items

Three of these are worth reading first

FL-014 — no market data anywhere

Every edge, CLV, hold and ROI field on this site is blank because there is nothing to compute them from. It is not a formatting gap: without a captured close, “our line beat theirs” is unprovable, so the gates that matter most (G-03, G-07) cannot be closed at all. The cheapest fix is our own timestamped captures (S-20), which cost nothing but discipline.

FL-002 — the roster capture reached only the active list

Official roster pages are paginated by status, and the IR/PUP/practice-squad segment was not reached in this session. Depth and “next man up” statements are therefore bounded: the absence of a name in the excerpt means nothing about the team. This is the single largest limit on the depth page.

FL-013 — there is no official depth chart to read

Both plausible official paths failed. Until that is settled (Q-07), “2nd/3rd string” is a projection built from snap share and experience, and any page that says otherwise is overclaiming. The depth page deliberately renders its own example as an alphabetical, admittedly non-depth ordering to keep that distinction visible.

Open question about the repository itself

Name and purpose. “FL2” is not defined anywhere in the repo (the original README was one line), and there is no statement of intended use, users, or whether this is personal research or a product. That was not invented here: the site describes what the brief asked for — collect, organise, analyse, originate lines — and every legal/compliance row is left as a question (Q-14, Q-15) rather than an assumption.
