Line origination: signals, gates, correlation

Decision this page serves: which signals get built into the baseline, and what each must survive before its output may be quoted or bet. signals · gates · leg-pairs in the correlation register ( with a measured ρ).

No market data exists in this repo. Therefore this page contains methods and directions, never a price, a hold, an ROI or an empirical correlation. The cells that would normally carry those numbers are blank and flagged (FL-014 → Q-10). Any figure shown here as “computed” is arithmetic on assumptions we state, reproducible with python3 -m engine.selftest.

The pipeline, in order

1 · Capture

official report + roster + schedule, each row with an as-of stamp, a source URL and a schema contract. Fail loudly on shape change (Q-18).

2 · Normalise

position crosswalk (FL-003), absence-reason taxonomy so rest ≠ injury (SIG-06), player registry keyed on the official permalink (SG-07).

3 · Availability state

per club, per position: who is Out/Doubtful, who is ACT, who is left, with the experience of the next man up (R-10).

4 · Baseline distribution

μ and σ for margin and each team total, frozen as v0 with a hash before any signal is layered on (SG-13).

5 · Signal adjustments

each adjustment is a named function with declared inputs; an undefined input propagates to “no quote”, never to a default value (SIG-22).

6 · Quote & compare

our number, a price at three quantiles (SG-14), the market’s open/close, and the residual — the only honest object of study.

7 · Combo pricing

joint distribution over legs via the copula; the book’s multiplier inverted into an implied ρ, and traded only when it disagrees with ours.

8 · Gate battery

G-01…G-16 produce pass / fail / unknown. Unknown never becomes pass by omission (implemented in engine/gates.py).

Signal catalogue

“Hypothesis” is the reason the market could be slow — a signal without one is a data column. blocked means a required input does not exist in our stack yet (it is not a rejection of the idea).

Validation gates — every strategy must clear all applicable gates

Thresholds are policy, held in engine/config.py so a change is a reviewed edit rather than a rebuild. A gate returns unknown when the evidence is missing; unknown blocks promotion.

What the gates say about luck (all values computed here)

Read the second table as a rule: if you try 100 variants, the best one needs a Sharpe above ≈0.51 per period to be more than the maximum of noise. If you do not record how many variants you tried, nobody can deflate anything — which is why the registry (SG-16) is a gate input and not a nicety.

Correlation handling

The combiner is a Gaussian copula over a shared latent game state. Two legs with marginals p₁, p₂ and correlation ρ:

P(both) = ∫ φ(z) · Πᵢ [ 1 − Φ( (Φ⁻¹(1−pᵢ) − √ρ·z) / √(1−ρ) ) ] dz

For two legs this is the exact bivariate normal tail (valid for negative ρ too, which the one-factor form cannot express — engine/parlay.py branches on that). For three or more legs it is exact when the pairwise correlation really is equal, and a stated approximation otherwise. Convergence to the Fréchet bounds is tested rather than assumed, because it is O(√(1−ρ)) — at ρ=0.999 a 0.55/0.55 pair reaches 0.5429, not 0.55.

The register, and why its value column is empty

Mechanism and sign can be argued without data (a reception is a component of receiving yards); a number cannot. Filling ρ with folklore is how a combo engine manufactures its own losses: the register therefore stores the measurement plan — simulation window, shrinkage toward 0, per-season stability — and refuses to quote any pair whose legs share a driver until it is populated (Q-12, R-19, G-16).

The reference engine

Layout

  • engine/odds.py — odds ⇄ probability, de-vig (multiplicative), break-even, EV, fractional Kelly, Wilson interval, “bets needed to prove a rate”.
  • engine/parlay.py — copula joint tails, parlay pricing, all-lose cluster probability, and implied_rho_from_parlay(), which inverts a book’s price into the ρ it assumes.
  • engine/stats.py — skew/kurtosis, SR*, the deflated Sharpe ratio, MinTRL, moving-block bootstrap and an exact sign-flip test.
  • engine/gates.py — the 16 gates, each returning pass / fail / unknown with a reason string.
  • engine/registry.py — pre-commitment log: canonical hash of a spec before its window opens, and the variant count G-04 needs.
  • engine/selftest.py — 46 assertions plus every “computed” figure on this site.

Verify it yourself

cd FL2 && python3 -m engine.selftest

The assertions are identities a reader can check with a calculator (e.g. −110 ⇒ implied 0.523809, de-vigged 0.500000; quarter-Kelly at p=0.55 ⇒ 0.01375 of bankroll), so trusting the engine is not required to audit it. Dry-run behaviour worth knowing: a deliberately weak record finishes 1/16 passed, 2 failed, 13 unknown → NOT PROMOTABLE, and the missing-evidence gates say so instead of defaulting to success.

What “selectively skill-based” means operationally

Selection is a rate, not a feeling

The VETO rule (SIG-22) makes “no bet” a produced outcome. Skill shows up as a higher edge per bet at a lower bet count; a strategy that qualifies on everything is measuring the market, not itself.

Skill is judged on pricing, PnL is judged on CLV

G-14 scores the number (Brier/log-loss/MAE vs the frozen baseline); G-03 scores the ticket (did we beat the close). PnL alone is on neither list, because a hot month satisfies it.

Availability signals must not be credited twice

SIG-01…05, 16, 17 all read the same absence event. Cluster ids (G-16) plus one-stake-per-cluster sizing is what stops a five-leg SGP from being one bet with five times the risk.
