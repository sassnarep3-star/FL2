What was already measured, and what today's check changes. The play rate per designation is already computed in this repository from 2016-2024 official-report rows joined to participation rows, with n and an interval on every row and the participation trap documented. What was missing was the primary source: three requirements in research/requirements-v2.md (R69, R78, R91) are recorded as blocked on the grounds that no archive of past weekly reports is reachable. The archive exists; the test behind that verdict was run against a mirror rather than against the league's own page. Today two past-season reports were rendered from the league's own site, so the calibration can be re-derived from the record itself and extended to 2025 and 2026. The numbers below are unchanged until that re-derivation is run - this page does not overwrite them with a hope.
