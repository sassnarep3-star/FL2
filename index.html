The two numbers that decide whether this project can work

You need 52.38% just to break even

At the standard −110 price, the implied win rate is 52.38%. Both sides sum to 104.76%, so the book keeps 4.76%. A strategy that wins 51% is losing money. Gate G5 exists for this.

2,243 bets to prove a 55% edge

To distinguish a true 55% win rate from a 52.38% break-even at 80% power needs 2,243 settled bets. Ranking strategies on a few hundred bets ranks luck. The arithmetic is here.

What is blocked, plainly

No odds or price data is in this repository. That single gap blocks gates G3 (out-of-sample profit), G4 (closing-line value), G5 (net-of-vig EV) and G9 (capacity) for every strategy. Nine of twelve strategies are blocked on it. This is a purchasing decision, not an engineering one — see Q15.
Depth charts exist for 2 of 32 clubs. The requirement to know whether a defence is on its 2nd, 3rd or 4th string is met for Kansas City and Seattle only, because those are the only official club depth charts captured in this pass. nfl.com/teams/{club}/depthchart returns 404 — there is no league-wide official endpoint on that path. The other 30 clubs are blank, not estimated. See Injuries & Depth.
Cross-sectional correlation is not betting correlation. The r values above describe how 32 teams relate to each other in one week. They do not tell you how two legs of a same-game parlay move together. Conflating the two is the most common way a project like this goes wrong. See the limits section.
