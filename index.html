How to read any number on this site

An em dash —

Means not confirmed from an applicable official source; it is never zero or an estimate. The review route says whether an official source path exists, the gap is a policy decision, or the item is only supported by local repository artifacts.

Badges are status, not opinion

A status badge describes the named section's own rule; it does not certify the whole repository. The intended leaderboard gate separates backtests from prospective results and does not rank on win rate or ROI alone; existing rankings remain exploratory until validated.

No prices without a source

The Week 10 comparison points to nflverse, not a sportsbook quote source. Price-source terms and redistribution policy are unresolved, so do not read it as an approved or current quote.
