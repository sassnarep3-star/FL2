A verified NFL dataset, a line model, and the case against pretending it works

Ten single-bet strategies were tested against 16 seasons of closing lines with eight validation gates each. None passed. A transparent Elo model scores Brier 0.2277 against the market's 0.2106 — 8.1% worse — which is exactly why. This hub publishes the verified data, the harness that produces that verdict in an afternoon, and an explicit register of what is still unknown.

Which one should you read? Read the decision-readiness table first — it states, requirement by requirement, what is settled and what is still blocked, then follow the numbers into the Evidence Ledger and the Strategy Lab. Six sections now carry verified or measured NFL data — the ledger (standings, points for and against, defensive yardage, the official injury report, 16 seasons of closing lines), Originated Lines (a 2026 results feed reconciled to the official standings, originated prices with residuals, designation rates measured on 23,389 player-weeks), FL2 Dashboard and Signal Lab, Verified Signals and Line Lab. Two of them — Originated Lines and Line Lab — originate the same markets from different models and disagree in places; both stay published rather than being merged, because a second opinion that is quietly deleted is not a check, and a reader comparing them learns more than from either alone. The remaining sections (docs, hub, and the framework pages at this root) deliberately left every NFL value blank. Their restraint was correct at the time; the reconciled data is what those frameworks were waiting for.

Three independent decision-readiness tables now exist — /fl2-lines/ (74 requirements, this session), /readiness/ and /linelab/, written by three sessions given the same brief. They are kept apart deliberately: where two of them disagree about a requirement's status, that disagreement is the finding, and collapsing them into one table would erase it. Consolidating the eight structures into one is proposed as a decision, not done silently. See Suggestions.

How to read any number on this site

An em dash —

Means not confirmed from an official source. It is never a zero and never an estimate. Every such cell is also on the Flagged page with the source that would fill it.

Badges are status, not opinion

validated requires 8/8 gates on out-of-sample data. rejected means it failed them. Nothing is currently green.

No prices without a source

Sportsbook prices have no official publisher. Market numbers come only from an aggregated closing-line file, are always labelled T3, and are never a model input.
