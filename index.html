Which one should you read? Read the Evidence Ledger. It is the only section that contains verified numeric NFL data — standings, points for and against, defensive yardage, the official injury report, seven quarterback decisions, and 16 seasons of closing lines with results. The other sections are well-built frameworks that deliberately left every NFL value blank. Their restraint was correct at the time; the ledger is what those frameworks were waiting for.

Consolidating the five structures into one is proposed as a decision, not done silently. See Suggestions.

How to read any number on this site

An em dash —

Means not confirmed from an official source. It is never a zero and never an estimate. Every such cell is also on the Flagged page with the source that would fill it.

Badges are status, not opinion

validated requires 8/8 gates on out-of-sample data. rejected means it failed them. Nothing is currently green.

No prices without a source

Sportsbook prices have no official publisher. Market numbers come only from an aggregated closing-line file, are always labelled T3, and are never a model input.
