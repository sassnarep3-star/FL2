Executive summary

What can be decided now?

We can choose a research and validation process, not a profitable strategy or current wager. The repository began with only a README; there is no historical odds feed, play-by-play dataset, roster/depth-chart history, bankroll policy, model, or validated result in the project. The decision-ready framework is to use NFL-origin statistics and availability as features; timestamp every input and price; calibrate win/cover/prop probabilities; compare against de-vigged market probabilities where an authorized odds source is chosen; and publish only strategies that clear pre-registered, time-ordered tests.

A future-looking “expected edge” is not a result. Nothing here claims that any signal beats a market or removes randomness.

How to use this dashboard

1. Review suggestions

Open Suggestions to answer explicit questions and choose next work only after blockers are resolved.

2. Inspect strategy gates

Read Strategy lab for a testable, falsification-first process and separate validation for singles, parlays, and props.

3. Do not fill blanks

Unknown values stay blank. The review register names the missing evidence and what would establish it.

Independent research scaffold, not an NFL product, betting recommendation, or guarantee. No wagering or live tracking is implemented. Source links lead to official NFL pages unless noted otherwise. Project verification log.
