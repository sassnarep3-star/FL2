What can be decided today, and what cannot

Decidable now (evidence in this repo)

  • Injury-report ingestion works and the field semantics are understood — including that an empty Game Status cell is normal, and that “not injury related – rest” rows must be excluded from injury features (verified in the official Week 10 report, such rows this week).
  • Practice-status and designation vocabularies are known, so the schema can be frozen now.
  • Replacement-depth measurement is possible from the official roster page’s experience field, and its limits are mapped (which positions the excerpt covers).
  • The validation arithmetic (de-vig, break-even, Wilson bounds, Kelly caps, copula parlay pricing, deflation for multiple testing) is written, tested and reproducible — see methodology.
  • The requirement, question and suggestion registers are complete enough to review and choose from.

Not decidable yet — and why

  • Any edge, ROI, CLV or hold figure: no odds or closing-line source exists (FL-014, Q-10). Publishing one would be fabrication.
  • Any measured correlation between parlay legs: the register lists mechanism and sign, values are blank until we can simulate or observe them (Q-12).
  • Depth ordering as fact: no official depth-chart endpoint was found (two guessed official paths 404’d — Q-07, FL-013), so “2nd/3rd string” stays a labelled projection.
  • Player-value inputs for usage-based props: play-by-play and snap counts are licensed data (Q-01, R-27).
  • Jurisdiction and operating model: state counts and Mexico permit/tax claims disagree between sources and no regulator text has been read (FL-010, FL-011, Q-14, Q-15).
  • Roster-state completeness (IR/PUP/practice squad): the capture reached only the active segment of the official roster page (FL-002).

How to read the numbers on this site

official-league

Published by the league office (nfl.com). Used for the injury report, records and roster status. This is the strongest tier present here.

official-club

The club’s own news desk (vikings.com, chargers.com). Carries per-day practice detail the league page collapses; used for cross-checks.

snippet

Reached only through a search extract, not re-opened. Quoted as “reported by”, never used as a model input until the page is opened directly.

secondary-aggregator

Trackers and commentary. Kept for lead-finding only; two of them disagree with each other on a legal count, which is exactly why they are not trusted for numbers.

computed (ours)

Arithmetic produced by engine/ in this repo. Reproducible, checkable, and carries no external authority.

blank

The value could not be confirmed from a source in its allowed tier. Blank plus a flag, by rule R-05 — not zero, not “probably”.
