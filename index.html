FL2 Dashboard

Collecting, organizing, and analyzing NFL requirements for line origination, signal generation, strategy validation, and injury / depth-deficiency tracking. Every verified data row links to its official source. Nothing is inferred — unverifiable values are left blank and flagged.

What this project covers

Verified data

  • 2026 standings — all 32 teams, from the official NFL standings page.
  • Week 10 schedule — every game, time, network, and result, from the official NFL schedule page.
  • Week 10 injury report — every listed player for all 28 playing teams, from the official NFL injury report.
  • Depth-chart tracking — starters and 2nd/3rd/4th-string backups on offense and defense, from official team sources.

Decision support

  • Executive summary — the important details in one place.
  • Questions & answers — the open questions and missing information that must be resolved before decisions.
  • Suggestions — improvement proposals to review and choose from before anything is implemented.
  • Strategies — signal-generation design: single bets, parlays, player-prop combos, validation gates, correlation, and luck removal.
  • Leaderboard — strategy competition with rewards for top performers.
Verification rule in force. Every table row carries a link to the official source used to verify it. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged on the Flagged for Review page. See Verification for the full method and fetch log.

How to use this site

  • Start with the Executive Summary for the current state of the project.
  • Review open decisions and missing information in Questions & Answers.
  • Review and rank the proposed changes in Suggestions — nothing is implemented until a path is chosen there.
  • Strategy designs and validation gates live in Strategies; registered strategies are scored on the Leaderboard.
  • Check Flagged for Review for irregularities and unverified items.
