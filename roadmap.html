Roadmap — what to build, in what order, and why

Decision this page serves: the sequence. Each phase states what it unblocks, so a phase can be cancelled on evidence rather than on enthusiasm. Nothing here is scheduled; ordering is by dependency, and the dependency is the missing data, not the effort.

Ordering rationale, in one sentence: phases 1–3 make the inputs trustworthy, 4 makes the model falsifiable, 5–6 make the market comparable, 7–8 make the competition honest, and 9–10 decide whether any of it may be used commercially — so starting at 9 is how a project like this fails.

Standing weekly loop once phases 1–3 exist

Tue–Wed

Capture practice reports; log clubs whose trajectory contradicts their designations (SIG-07, FL-009). Recompute dOAV/dDAV priors; no quotes yet.

Thu–Fri

Final designation capture; publish our number per market with a quantile band; record the residual against the market at a fixed checkpoint (Q-11). Re-price on any post-deadline revision (G-15).

Sat–Sun

Inactive lists → diff against designations. Grade outcomes; append to the bet ledger with cluster ids. No new rules are written mid-week; only the registry grows.

Mon

Recompute gates and the leaderboard; promote/bench mechanically; write the decision log entry for anything the arithmetic changed.

Monthly

Re-read 10% of published table rows against their sources (Q-19); publish the disagreement rate on the verification page. Prune dead sources, add newly discovered ones.

Per window

Re-fit nothing on the confirmatory folds; retire signals that fail G-08/G-14 twice in a row rather than re-parameterising them until they pass.

What we are deliberately not doing

No bet placement or automation

Execution raises licensing and terms-of-use questions (Q-15) that no amount of modelling answers. Origination is the deliverable.

No “live” data on the site

A static snapshot with visible age is auditable; a page that fetches at load time is not reproducible by a reviewer (FL-015).

No model complexity before a baseline

Every additional free parameter must justify itself on a holdout (G-05). That is a refusal to start with a gradient-boosted everything-model, on purpose.

No leaderboard numbers before G-01

An empty board is honest; a populated one built on 30 bets is a machine for rewarding luck (Q-13, Q-22).

No inferred cells, ever

Blank plus flag is the rule (R-05). It makes tables look incomplete and makes the site trustworthy; the trade is not close.

No scraping of restricted content at scale

Until Q-01 is answered, this remains a small attributed snapshot rather than a mirror of the league’s data.
