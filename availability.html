Depth and backup deficiency

Decision this page serves: whether an absence is absorbed by the next man up or actually changes the unit. “2nd, 3rd, 4th string” is only meaningful if it is measured from something; here it is measured from roster status and years of experience on the official roster page, and everything the official data cannot support is left blank and flagged rather than assumed.

The honest limitation, stated first. No official, machine-readable depth chart was found: two plausible official paths returned a 404 and a broken-link page (FL-013, S-06 / S-07). So this page does not claim anyone is a “second-stringer”. It claims something weaker and checkable: given a named absence from the official report, which other players at that position are on the club’s active roster, and what their published experience is.

Worked example — MIN @ GB, Week 10 (the only fixture with both feeds captured)

The two features this feeds

offence Offensive availability delta (dOAV)

Absences at QB / RB / WR / TE / C / G / T. Weighted by protection and touch concentration rather than by name value, because the observable failure mode of a backup offensive line is *sustained pressure and reduced rush rate*, which shows up in team-total and rush-prop prices before it shows up in the spread.

Verified instance in this snapshot: Green Bay lists three offensive absences among linemen and the leading rusher Out (Jacobs — Finger — Out, Rhyan — Ankle — Out, Zeitler — Ankle, Triceps — Questionable). The captured active-roster excerpt shows the backfield replacements at Kaleb Johnson (2 yr) and MarShawn Lloyd (3 yr) — that is the deficiency signal, stated as data.

defence Defensive availability delta (dDAV)

Absences at DE / DT / LB / CB / S, weighted by position leverage (corner and edge rusher before nose tackle) and by whether the *replacement* is a specialist or a convert. A defence missing two starters in the same back-seven unit changes the down distribution, which is a totals fact as much as a side fact.

Verified instance: Green Bay lists Parsons — Knee, Illness — Out, Franklin — Shin — Doubtful, Van Ness — Back — Questionable with McKinney and Bullard Questionable in the secondary: four of the ten defensive names on the report carry a Friday limitation, and the only measured back-up quality available is 3 years (Hopper) and 6 years (McDuffie, Niemann) at linebacker on the captured roster excerpt.

Formula (as implemented, with refusal rules)

dU = Σabsent i w(posi) · snapshare(posi, i)  ·  [ value(i) − value(replacementi) ] where value() is currently only years of experience + roster status, both officially available.

  • Refusal rule 1 (SG-12): if two or more starters at one position are Out, the unit is marked projection-invalid and no adjustment is published. The model has no support for that state and must say so.
  • Refusal rule 2: if the replacement cannot be identified from a captured roster segment, value(replacement) is undefined and propagates — no positional average is silently substituted.
  • Refusal rule 3 (SIG-06): rows whose injury text contains “Not injury related” are dropped from dU entirely, and instead counted in a separate rest feature.
  • Upgrade path: snapshare is the term we cannot compute yet — it needs play-by-play data (R-27, Q-01). Until then dU is a *count-and-weight* index, not a points-equivalent, and must not be multiplied into a scoring model as if it were one.

Source data behind the example

Official club roster pages as published; the captured segment is the active list in alphabetical order, so absence of a name below is a capture limit, not a claim about the team ( FL-002).

Design decisions taken here

Why experience, when it is a crude proxy

It is the only player-quality-adjacent field the league publishes on the same page as roster status, so it inherits that page’s verification. A model built on a sharper but unsourced proxy would look better and be less auditable. It is explicitly a starting value scale, not an end one.

Why “alphabetically first at the position” instead of a depth guess

Because it is reproducible from the captured file and obviously not a depth claim — which keeps the demonstration honest while the ordering source is still an open question. When a real ordering exists, this function is the one that changes.
