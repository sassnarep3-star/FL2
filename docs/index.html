What this project is

A line-by-line transcription of the 2026 NFL season from official sources, plus the analytics machinery to test betting strategies against it. Two rules are absolute: every table row links to the official page it came from, and any value that cannot be confirmed is left blank rather than filled in. There is no inferred number anywhere on this site.

Headline findings

  • Home-field advantage is approximately zero in 2026. Home teams won 48.91% of 137 games and the mean home margin is −0.022 points (SD 13.225). The 95% CI on the mean is roughly [−2.24, +2.20], so this is not proof that HFA disappeared — it is proof that 137 games cannot measure it. Any model hard-coding +2.5 home points is wrong for this season.
  • Every strategy tested fails its validation gate. The best walk-forward model scores 56.19% straight-up against a 52.38% break-even, but its Brier skill score is +0.0017 — i.e. zero. It is published as rejected, with the arithmetic.
  • A season leaderboard cannot detect a 55% bettor. Detecting 55% at α=.05 with 80% power needs 2,243 bets (about 11 seasons). Over 200 bets the same bettor has p = 0.208.
  • Club depth charts lag the official injury report. 12 players are listed 1st/2nd string on a club chart while carrying Out/Doubtful on the NFL's own report; 3 Out players appear nowhere on their own club's chart.
  • Parlay prices move enormously with correlation. At 3 legs, ρ = 0.30 versus ρ = 0 changes the fair price by 52%. No ρ has been estimated from data, so the site publishes a sensitivity grid instead of a single number.
