Unit exposure - counts only

For each team and position group: how many entries are listed and how many are Out, Doubtful or Questionable. These are descriptive counts from the NFL.com report. They do not identify starters or backups, which is not yet verified (F-10).

"Flag" is a simple review rule, not a prediction: any QB Out or Doubtful, or 2+ Out or Doubtful in one unit. "Not injury related" entries are excluded from the status counts.
