Validation gates

The numeric bars every strategy must clear

Validation Gates

Every strategy must clear these gates before it is allowed onto the leaderboard. A gate is a number, not an opinion. All gates are computed in tools/analytics.py and stored in data/derived/analytics.json.

Gate definitions

GateRuleWhy it exists
G0 — Data provenanceEvery input row traces to an official URL; no inferred or imputed valuesKills fabricated inputs before they reach a model
G1 — No look-aheadA game's prediction may use only games with an earlier week numberThe most common way a backtest lies
G2 — Out-of-sample hit rate vs break-evenWin rate must exceed the break-even rate for the price at the sample size achievedYou cannot claim profit below break-even
G3 — Statistical significanceTwo-sided p ≤ 0.05 against the break-even rateRemoves luck (requirement 4)
G4 — Proper scoring ruleBrier skill score > 0 against the base-rate null, *and* log-loss better than the nullA hit rate can look good by picking only favourites; a proper score cannot
G5 — Side balanceThe model must not pick one side in >70% of graded games unless that side's base rate justifies itDirectly from the pathology found in the walk-forward model (see below)
G6 — Correlation honestyAny parlay price must state the ρ used, or publish a sensitivity grid instead of a point estimatePrevents fake precision in parlay pricing
G7 — Sample-size disclosureReport the n required for 80% power at the claimed edgeMakes the "is this luck?" question answerable

The pathology that produced gate G5

The free-intercept model picked the home side 78.1% of the time in a season where home teams win 48.91%. It therefore scored below the base rate it was implicitly betting, while the identical model with the intercept pinned at zero scored 56.19% on the same games. Gate G5 now exists so this failure mode cannot be published again in good faith.
