2. The important details, stated plainly

a. The injury report is richer than it looks — and full of traps

  • An empty Game Status cell is normal, not missing data: a Friday designation is only required for players who did not practise fully. This snapshot has such rows out of .
  • “Not injury related – resting player” appears in the official injury column ( rows this week). Feed those into an injury feature and you will penalise clubs for resting healthy veterans — the single easiest way to build a confidently wrong availability model.
  • Designations and practice status can contradict each other in both directions (limited practice yet Out; did-not-participate and resting yet Questionable) — see FL-009.
  • The club page and the league page agree on status but differ on granularity; they also disagree internally on one injury label (FL-004).

b. “Who replaces them” is only half-sourced

  • The official roster page supplies status, position and years of experience — enough to measure an experience cliff when a starter is out.
  • No official depth-chart endpoint was found: two plausible official paths returned a 404 and a broken-link page (FL-013). Any 1st/2nd/3rd-string claim is therefore a projection and must be labelled as one.
  • The roster page is paginated by status; the active segment was captured, the IR/PUP/practice-squad segment was not (FL-002). “No players on IR” would be a capture artefact, not a fact.
  • Roster movement is fast enough to break cached data: a QB listed on one club’s 2026 report was on a different club’s roster list last season (FL-008).

c. Luck, not effort, is the binding constraint

Computed by the engine, not asserted:

Bets needed for the lower 90% Wilson bound to clear the −110 break-even of 52.381%. A 58% season on 100 bets is not evidence; that is why gates G-01…G-16 exist and why the leaderboard is empty.

d. Correlation is the parlay — and it is unreadable today

  • Books price parlays by applying a multiplier to the independent product; inverting that multiplier gives an implied ρ (0.55/0.52 legs at ρ=+0.4 → fair decimal 2.849 vs 3.497 naive, a 1.227× joint-probability lift). That is the whole same-game-parlay trade.
  • House rules void correlated legs outside SGP-type products, void duplicate-market parlays, and settle pushes by reducing legs — so a mathematically fine ticket can be void by policy (markets page).
  • Measured ρ for all 12 leg-pairs in the register: none. Nothing in this repo can estimate them yet, because there is no market data source ( FL-014).

4. The five actions that unlock the most

  1. Close the market-data gap (Q-10). Start self-recording line snapshots at T-48h / T-24h / T-2h / close for every game modelled. Nothing about edge, CLV, gates G-03/G-07, or the leaderboard can be computed before this exists, and the free version is ours to own.
  2. Fix the roster-state capture (R-07, FL-002). Capture the full roster page including IR/PUP/NFI/practice-squad segments for all 32 clubs, then the depth model has a denominator.
  3. Settle the ordering question (Q-07). Either find the working official per-club path or adopt snap-share ordering from a licensed feed and publish it explicitly as a projection.
  4. Automate the ingest (SG-30, R-39). The snapshot in this repo is the regression fixture: the crawler must reproduce it exactly, then extend it to the full slate and all three practice days.
  5. Answer the rights question (Q-01, Q-14, Q-15). League-data licensing plus operating jurisdiction decide whether this is internal research or a product; everything else is downstream of that.
