How the ranking works (so it can be argued with)

Impact I
how much it changes the quality of a decision
0–5, judgement, stated openly
Confidence C
how much evidence in this repo backs the claim
0–5; 5 = a source or a test, 1 = plausibility
Effort E
cost to build, 1 = one file
1–5
Risk R
harm if the suggestion is wrong
0–5; irreversibility counts double

Priority is computed by the page, not typed by hand: (I + C) × 2 − E − R. Filter and sort the table below to re-rank it under your own weights — change what you believe, and the order changes with it. Anything you disagree with should become a rejection with a reason in the decision log (SG-03), not silence.
