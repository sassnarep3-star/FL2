Decision readiness

Executive summary (session 310620fe, 2026-11-16). The verified results corpus now covers 148 finals (137 of them independently cross-verified against two sources, 0 mismatches). Home-field advantage measures −0.65 pts — approximately zero again. The luck panel puts every team's win total within ±2.2 wins of its Pythagorean expectation (1σ ≈ 1.5 at n≈9). Control baselines are frozen (favourites cover 52.0%; random 54.1%). STRAT-1 is the first pre-registered strategy: method text frozen 2026-11-16, three days before Week 11 opens, with 13 forecasts and their market residuals recorded pre-kickoff. Infrastructure now exists for the price-snapshot store (openings blank — no reachable source publishes them), the point-in-time snapshot ledger, drift alarms on seven datasets, Wed/Fri availability capture automation, the venue feed (Munich cross-verified against the official game centre) and the per-team availability view. Remaining blockers are all source-access problems (IR/reserve status, NWS weather, archived injury seasons, play-level 2026 data) and are listed with their missing sources on Flagged for Review.

One row per requirement in the current brief (R26–R96; the original R01–R25 register lives in data/requirements.csv). Each row states the gate that blocks it and the number that would clear it. Statuses were set from files that exist in the repository and from live official-page fetches on 2026-11-16.
