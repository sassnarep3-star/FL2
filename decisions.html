Suggestions & open decisions

This section separates what is already known from the questions that block a safe, testable product. Answers below are limited to the repository and verified official source pathways; no user preference is guessed.

Recommendation: approve a source-and-data feasibility phase first, then a simulated forecasting competition. Do not start with cash rewards, live wagers, public “best bets,” or automated bets. Until market data access, rights, risk rules and validation are settled, those paths cannot be responsibly measured or implemented.

Suggested implementation order

Phase A · source feasibility

Prove the inputs first

Confirm rights, coverage, historical depth, timestamps and data shape for official NFL stats/injury/schedule records and for an authorized market feed. Deliver a source register and data quality report; no picks.

Phase B · forecast lab

Immutable evaluation

Build baselines, pre-register candidate signals, preserve time-ordered train/validation/test splits, report probability calibration and uncertainty, and keep every rejected experiment in the research log.

Phase C · simulated competition

Reward evidence, not noise

Run frozen prospective paper forecasts. Publish separate classes for singles, parlays and prop combos; rank only after minimum sample and consistency gates are defined. Seek legal/compliance review before any non-simulated reward.
