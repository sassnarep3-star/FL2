Questions that gate a decision

Decision this page serves: what must be known before we commit build effort or money. Each row states what we have (with its source), what we do not have, the concrete way to find out, and the provisional answer being used meanwhile. If the “How to find out” column is vague, that is the bug — not the question.

Reading rule. A provisional answer is a working assumption, not a finding. Everything built on one is marked as such in the requirement register and in the signal catalogue, and each provisional answer is written so it can be falsified by a single action.

The register

Analysis: three clusters, three different owners

1. Rights and jurisdiction (Q-01, Q-14, Q-15)

Owner decision, because it is a spend and a legal posture, not a technical unknown. These bound every other answer: an ingestion design that assumes licensed data is a different design from one that assumes self-recorded snapshots.

2. Data existence (Q-05, Q-07, Q-10, Q-16)

Mostly resolvable by the agent: open the right page, capture it, diff it. The only reason they are open is that the primary sources were not reached in this session — not because they are unknowable.

3. Method choices that change the maths (Q-08, Q-11, Q-12, Q-13)

These need a decision before results are computed, otherwise the choice is made by whichever benchmark flatters the outcome. Freezing them now is the cheapest integrity control available.
