Operating principles

  • Every raw record should retain the source URL and retrieval time; corrected records should add a new version, not silently replace history.
  • Use only features known before the forecast/market cutoff; avoid future injury status, final inactives, game outcomes, or closing lines in earlier predictions.
  • Separate source facts, derived variables, model estimates, and editorial suggestions in storage and UI.
  • Document definitions, denominators, exclusions, missingness and join keys before computing rates or correlations.
  • Revalidate after rule changes and roster/role shifts; do not carry a factor across eras without an out-of-sample test.
