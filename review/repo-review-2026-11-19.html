What was re-checked against official nfl.com pages, what was corrected, what was flagged, and what was not checked. Read this before relying on any Week 10 number in the repository.

Scope, stated plainly. This is a targeted re-verification, not a complete line-by-line audit. Every row below carries a source link. Anything not listed in section 1 or section 2 is not verified by this review; section 5 names the largest gaps.

Executive summary

  • Week 10 results: all 14 official finals were re-read. Twelve games agree with every repository feed. Two rows are stale: Chargers 23–20 Ravens (not final in any feed) and Steelers 18–38 Bengals (blank in the fl2-lines feed). The corrections are logged for owner acceptance, not edited in place, because the pre-registered Week 11 forecast reads one of the feeds.
  • A false claim was corrected. The landing page said the data was captured before Week 10 kicked off. The Thursday Week 10 game was final two days before the capture date.
  • A false flag was corrected. The verified standings file marks all 32 teams as mismatched, but published net points equal points for minus points against for every team. Values are unchanged; only the note text was fixed.
  • Pre-registration timing holds on the evidence available. The Week 11 ledger was recorded 2026-11-16 01:30Z; the first Week 11 game kicks off 2026-11-20 01:15Z (8:15 PM ET, Nov 19).
  • Automated capture is fail-closed. NFL Terms §1.3 bars systematic retrieval to compile a database without written consent. Main has fail-closed guards (PR #45). No capture was run here. Authorisation is an owner decision (D1).
  • The main open items are owner decisions (section 4): capture authorisation, the Week 10 correction, a canonical injury file, large files in git, and site consolidation.

Requirement register (R26–R96, requirements_v2.json, readiness page): 29 done, 25 partial, 8 registered, 7 blocked, 2 rejected. These counts are the repository's own, not re-verified here.
