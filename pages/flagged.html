How to review this page

  1. Read the What is unverified column. If any of it matters to your decision, the What would resolve it column is the shopping list.
  2. For items with a source link, open it and confirm the absence or ambiguity directly. These are not claims that require trust — they are checkable in a browser.
  3. For F08 and F09 there is nothing to check: they are decisions, not facts, and they are yours to make.
  4. Nothing here invalidates the published data. It delimits it.
