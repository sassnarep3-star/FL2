Why the game-status column is the only one that matters

The official policy requires clubs to file a practice report on a fixed weekday pattern and a game status report the day before the game, with three designations: Out, Doubtful and Questionable. Practice participation is diagnostic of those designations, not a substitute for them. A player who is "Full Participation" with no game status is on the report and is not expected to miss time; a player who is "Questionable" might play or might not.

This matters for modelling because the labels have very different information content. The league defines the labels. It does not publish their predictive value, so any weighting we apply is our own and is labelled as such on the gates page. Calibrating it needs realised snap counts, which we do not have (Q08).

Worked example — where the data refuses to answer

Kansas City's defensive tackle Peter Woods is Out (neck) and sits at 2nd string behind Chris Jones. The next man listed is Marcus Harris, at 3rd string. So Kansas City is down a rotational tackle and is now on its 3rd string at that slot — a real, verified finding.

Contrast with Omarr Norman-Lott, also listed Out (knee) at defensive tackle. He does not appear at any string on the captured depth chart. Rather than guess where he would have been, the string cell is blank and the basis column says so. This is the blank rule in action: a missing answer is reported as missing.

How this would be extended to all 32 clubs

  1. Capture each club's official depth chart from its own site on a fixed schedule. They are published, structured, and each is labelled "unofficial depth chart" by the club itself — that caveat belongs on the page, not buried.
  2. Join on player name plus listed position. The join is the fragile part; it needs a player identifier, not a name.
  3. Add realised snap counts so the string can be validated against who actually played, which is also what calibrates the designation weights (Q08).
  4. Re-run daily during the season. A Week 10 snapshot is stale within hours once practice reports change.

This is suggestion SG09 and decision question Q09. Nothing here is inferred; where the document does not answer, the field is empty.
