Retrieval constraint, disclosed

The bash sandbox has no outbound network access except to GitHub, npm and PyPI. All web verification in this pass was therefore performed through a page-fetch tool against the official URLs, and the raw text of every fetched page was inspected before anything was transcribed. Data was never taken from memory, and no value was filled in because it "seemed right". The consequence for a reviewer: each table cell can be checked by opening the linked source URL, but it cannot be re-fetched by a script inside this environment.

Flagged for manual review

These are not errors in our work — they are gaps, ambiguities and negative findings that a human should look at. They are all published, with source links, on the Flagged for Review page and none of them blocked the merge.

  1. 30 of 32 clubs have no verified depth chart, so 2nd/3rd/4th-string analysis is impossible for them.
  2. No official league-wide depth-chart endpoint exists at the path one would expect (404 confirmed).
  3. No odds or price data exists anywhere in the project.
  4. Two clubs' published PPG differ from our derived PPG by 0.05 — rounding artefact, recorded rather than hidden.
  5. The league publishes injury designations but not their predictive value.
  6. NGS EPA values were only available for 16 of 32 clubs on the published page, so EPA columns are blank for the other 16.
  7. Prediction-market engagement is treated by the league as covered by its gambling policy, which affects whether an auto-betting product is viable at all.
