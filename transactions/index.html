1 · Depth charts vs. official roster events

Rule. A club depth chart carries a capture date, not a page date. An official roster event that names a player on that chart and is dated after the capture means the chart row cannot reflect it, so the row is flagged stale. A same-day event has unknown order and is not counted. An earlier event is not cleared, because the chart's own date is not published.

Scope. Only the 5 of 32 clubs whose depth charts are held (NE, DET, GB, KC, SF). Baltimore and Indianapolis depth charts are not held, so the Baltimore and Colts items cannot be tested.

3 · Irregularities for review

  • The monthly reserve-list pages each display exactly 25 rows, and October's begins at 10/24. This looks like a display cap. Appending ?page=2 returns the same page. Completeness is therefore unverified.
  • The official reserve-list pages publish no position for any row.
  • The NFL publishes no official depth chart. The per-club nfl.com depth URL returns 404; the club charts are captioned “Unofficial” by the clubs.
  • Depth-chart pages carry no date of their own (page_date: null), so staleness is judged against the capture date.
  • Terms §1.3 gate: row-level capture of NFL.com content is held private until the owner records consent or a licensed source is found (Flagged F-T1).
