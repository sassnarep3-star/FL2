1. Official Week 10 injury report — team-level view (captured 2026-11-16)

Source: the official NFL.com injury report, Week 10 of the 2026 season, transcribed on 2026-11-16 from all eight page chunks and checked by tools/official_injury_capture.py. Counts only are shown on this page.

Row-level player data is not published here. The NFL.com terms of use (section 1.3) prohibit systematic retrieval of content to compile a database without written consent. Player rows are held in a git-ignored private store until that is resolved — see Flagged F-T1. Each club below links to the official report, where every player row can be checked directly. Earlier row-level tables (2026-11-14 capture) are superseded by this view.

The official report does not state which players are starters, so this page cannot name starters out or replacements. That requires a verified depth-chart source for each club (see Q-section and Flagged F-D1).
