Strategy Leaderboard

The competition that rewards top-performing strategies. A strategy appears here only after passing all validation gates (see Strategies) and being scored with the luck-removed skill metrics. The table is intentionally empty — no strategy has passed the gates yet, and no unverified scores are published.

1. Rules (proposed — pending approval via S6)

RuleProposed specificationStatus
Eligibility A strategy must pass validation gates G1–G9 (Strategies §4) before its score is published. Pending approval
Scoring metric Variance-stabilized blended skill score: closing-line value, calibration, selective edge, and shrinkage toward baseline (Strategies §6). Raw win rate is not the ranking metric. Pending approval
Season scope Scores accumulate over the NFL regular season, with rolling sub-period standings so regime changes are visible. Pending approval
Ranking Strategies ranked by blended skill score, minimum bet-count requirement enforced (gate G2). Pending approval
Diversification Strategies above the correlation cap (gate G6) are flagged and not double-counted in reward eligibility. Pending approval
Tie-breaks Higher selective-edge on the high-conviction subset, then higher calibration score. Pending approval
Rewards Reward tiers for top-ranked strategies, with eligibility restricted to strategies passing all gates. The reward structure (tiers, amounts, payout cadence) is part of the S6 decision. Pending approval
Risk controls Bankroll and staking rules per S10; strategy de-listing if it violates G8 (selection-bias) or G9 (execution) rules. Pending approval
