Where can verified 2026 standings be obtained? The official NFL standings page provides all 32 teams with W-L-T, PCT, PF, PA, net points, streaks, and last-5. nfl.com/standings Answered
Where can verified Week 10 schedule data be obtained? The official NFL schedule page provides all Week 10 games (dates, times, networks), the final Thursday result (WAS 24, NYG 21), and bye teams (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB). nfl.com/schedules Answered
Where can verified injury designations be obtained? The official NFL injury report provides every listed player for each team with position, injury, practice status, and game status (Out / Questionable / Doubtful). nfl.com/injuries Answered
Where can official depth charts (1st/2nd/3rd/4th string) be obtained? Each team's official website publishes a depth chart (verified example: Detroit Lions). Teams label these "unofficial" depth charts compiled by team communications. detroitlions.com depth chart Answered Label caveat

Do official NFL sources publish betting lines or odds? No. The NFL does not publish betting lines. Lines and odds are published by licensed sportsbooks — a separate source class that must be selected and verified (see Q1). nfl.com Answered
