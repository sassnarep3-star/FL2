Executive Summary

Status of the FL2 project as of 2026-11-14. All data facts below are verified against official sources with links; design proposals are clearly labeled as proposals.

1. Project objective

Collect, organize, and analyze a full list of requirements for originating NFL lines. The program researches and develops strategies for generating useful betting signals, treating strategy performance as a leaderboard competition with rewards for top performers. Strategies cover single bets, parlays, and player-prop combinations. Every strategy must pass validation gates; correlation values between signals must be determined; and strategies must be built to be selectively skill-based with luck removed from the evaluation. The system tracks injuries and deficiencies in offenses and defenses — including situations where 2nd-, 3rd-, or 4th-string backups must replace starters.

4. Strategy program — design status

  • Signal generation — a candidate signal framework (market-derived baselines, injury-adjusted situational signals, and depth-deficiency signals) is designed on the Strategies page. It is a proposal, not yet validated.
  • Validation gates — every strategy must pass sample-size, stationarity, out-of-sample, and correlation gates before it can enter the leaderboard. Gate definitions are proposed on the Strategies page.
  • Luck removal — strategies are scored on skill-based metrics (closing-line value style measures, calibration, and selective edge) rather than raw win rate. Definitions are proposals pending the answers to the open questions.
  • Leaderboard — the competition table and reward rules are specified on the Leaderboard page. No strategies have been registered yet, because no strategy has passed the gates and no verified line/odds feed is in place yet.
