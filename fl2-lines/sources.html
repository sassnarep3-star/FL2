Verification method, in order

  1. Reproduce, don't assert. Every page is generated from CSV/JSON written by compute_results.py, compute_lines.py and compute_scores.py; re-running them must produce the committed files.
  2. Reconcile to the official page. The results feed is summed back into the published standings and compared for all 32 clubs; the check fails the build on any mismatch.
  3. Prove the sign conventions. The spread-line convention is tested against the moneyline sign and against the cover rate on 3,031 graded games; the model's own sanity is tested by requiring corr(model, market) > 0.55.
  4. Refuse to fill gaps. Blank scores for unconfirmed results, blank replacements, blank opening prices, blank depth cells; each is labelled rather than estimated.
  5. Keep the tiers apart. T1 official, T2 mirror of official, T3 aggregated market. A T3 number is never cited as a fact about a game and never mixes into the official correlation panel.
Reuse terms are unread for S01–S05 and S08–S11. That is why raw row-level capture stays outside the published pages and only derived counts, hashes and aggregates are shown (see data layer).
