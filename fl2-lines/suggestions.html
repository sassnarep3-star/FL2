rejected proposalwhy
Derive depth order from jersey number, experience or salaryIt is not a proxy for a starter; it is a proxy for a roster-page sort. The club charts already show multiple rank-1 players in one slot.
Rank strategies on season-long realised returnA season of singles is a few hundred bets; on that sample a 45% bettor can finish first. Ranking is on benchmark-relative skill, and ROI is an escrow measure.
Subtract a fixed points penalty for a missing starterThe measured effect is -0.5174 points per expected miss with a CI of -0.7357 to -0.2992; a fixed 3.0 is outside it by many standard errors.
