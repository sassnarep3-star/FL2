Week 10 2026 Slate

All 14 Week 10 games in official kickoff order with venues, results and a clearly-labelled rating differential that is not a line.

All 14 games of Week 10 2026 in official kickoff order, with venue, result where played, and a naive rating differential computed from verified team metrics. This is not a line and is not for sale.

What the margin column is, and is not. It is (home PPG − home PAPG) + (away PAPG − away PPG) — a pure form differential from nfl.com standings. It contains no home-field term, no injury adjustment, and no market information, and it is not a spread. A real originated line needs all three, and the market term needs an odds feed this repository does not have (Q01). It is shown because it is honest about what it is, and because it demonstrates the mechanics of line origination step by step.

What an actual originated line would add

  1. A home-field term. League-wide home advantage is a real, measurable quantity. It requires multi-season results, which are not ingested here. Without it, every number in the margin column is systematically short for the home team.
  2. An availability adjustment. The injury data exists (see Injuries & Depth) but the depth strings that would size the adjustment exist for 2 clubs only.
  3. A market comparison. A line with nothing to compare against is an opinion. The comparison against the close is the entire product.

Steps 1 and 3 are the blockers, and both are data purchases, not code.
