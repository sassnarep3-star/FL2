Negative findings

S17 — nfl.com/teams/{club}/depthchart returns HTTP 404. This is recorded as a source precisely because it establishes an absence: there is no league-wide official depth chart at the path a reasonable person would try first. That is why depth data covers 2 clubs instead of 32, and why Q09 exists. Recording a negative result is how a gap stays visible instead of quietly becoming a guess.
