Decision Questions

What we know, what we do not know, and exactly what would answer it — before any decision is made.

Eighteen questions. Each one states what we already know, what we specifically do not know, and the concrete artefact that would settle it. None of them is rhetorical: if a question cannot be answered by an artefact we could obtain, it does not belong on this list.

Read this as a blocker list, not a wish list. Answer Q15 and Q05 and most of the rest becomes mechanical. Q11 is the one that decides whether the project has a thesis at all.
