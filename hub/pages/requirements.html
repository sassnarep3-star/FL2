How status is assigned

StatusMeansTest you can apply
doneThe artefact exists, is published, and every number in it is sourced or blank.Open the page; click a source link; check the blank rule held.
partialPart of the requirement is satisfied; the remainder is blocked by missing data, stated explicitly.The gap is named on the page and appears on Flagged for Review.
proposedA method exists. No validation has been performed. No result is claimed.Search the page for a performance claim. There should be none.
