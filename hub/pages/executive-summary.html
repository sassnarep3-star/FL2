Executive Summary

The whole project in one page: verified facts, our own measurements, the real unknowns, and the three decisions that unblock the rest.

One page. What was asked, what is now verified, what is measured, what is genuinely unknown, and the three decisions that unblock the rest.

1. The ask, in six parts

  1. A full requirements list, organised and traceable.
  2. Suggestions for improving the site's decision quality, in a reviewable section, before anything is implemented.
  3. A clear list of questions, information and answers needed before a decision — including which information we do not have.
  4. Original NFL lines, built from signals, run as a rewarded leaderboard competition across single bets, parlays and prop combos, with validation gates, correlation values, and luck removed.
  5. Injury tracking that knows when a defence or offence is down to its 2nd, 3rd or 4th string.
  6. A verified GitHub Pages site, an executive summary, and a merged pull request — with no manual input.

All six are represented in the table below and traceable to a page. Requirements are in Requirements; the full register is data/requirements.csv.

3. What is measured (our own arithmetic)

MeasurementValueWhy it matters to a decision
Two-way hold at −110 / −1104.76% This is the tax. Any claimed edge smaller than this is not an edge.
Break-even win rate at −11052.38% A 53% strategy is profitable. A 52% strategy is not. The gap is one point wide.
Effective hold on a 2-leg −110 parlay8.88% Parlays multiply the tax, not the edge. 6 legs = 24.36%.
EV swing from correlation ρ = −0.40 → +0.40 on a 2-leg parlay -32.75% → 14.99% Assuming legs are independent is a ~47-point error. Correlation is not a refinement; it is the whole model.
Bets needed to prove a true 55% rate2,243 This is the leaderboard eligibility question, and it is arithmetic, not opinion.
Chance one zero-edge strategy shows ≥55% over 200 bets25.14% Across 500 such strategies, at least one looking like a genius is a near-certainty. That is the false-crown problem.
Offensive EPA/play vs PPGr = 0.926 R² 85.8%. Official efficiency is a good team rating — not yet a bet signal.
Rushing Y/A vs PPGr = 0.023 R² 0.1%. The data refused a popular hypothesis, which is the point of having data.

4. What is genuinely unknown

Everything about price. There is no odds archive, no closing line, no prop price and no book of record in this repository. Without it, no strategy can be graded, so none is claimed to work. 6 of 12 strategies are blocked on exactly this.
  • Whether the market is beatable at all. The published literature disagrees: Levitt (2004) argues books price to maximise profit rather than to balance; Boulier et al. (2006) could not reject efficiency; a ten-season home-underdog study found no variation profitable in every season. This is the project's largest single risk.
  • Whether a "Questionable" tag predicts a snap count. 84 of 160 status rows are Questionable. The league publishes the label, not its predictive value. Our proposed weights are guesses and are labelled as guesses.
  • Same-game correlation between two players. Not derivable from team tables; it needs player-level logs from both sides of the same game.
  • Depth strings for 30 clubs. Not published in a single official place we can reach.

5. The three decisions that unblock everything else

D1 · Buy a licensed historical odds archive

One purchase unblocks gates G3, G4, G5 and G9 for nine strategies. Nothing else in this project has that leverage. Answers Q01, Q02, Q03 and Q15 at once.

blocks 9 of 12 strategies

D2 · Pre-register the strategy registry and set a sample floor

Free, immediate, and it is what makes a leaderboard mean anything. Recommend: show at 500 bets, rank at 2,000, and correct for multiple comparisons. Answers Q04 and Q05.

zero cost, highest governance value

D3 · Decide what this is: research, product, or syndicate

The brief mixes all three. The recommended default is a research artefact that publishes originated lines and explicitly does not place bets — which also sidesteps the league's gambling-policy exposure and any licensing question. Answers Q17.
