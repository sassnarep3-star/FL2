Open questions

What has to be answered before depth is used in a model

These are not opinions. Each is a question with a specific way to find the answer, and none of them can be answered by reasoning — they need data that has not been captured yet.

Questions, why each blocks, and how to discover the answer
#QuestionWhat it blocksHow to find outStatus
1How far back do the Game Books go, and is the playtime table present in every season?A depth model needs several seasons, not one week. If older books lack the playtime table, the depth-from-playing-time route covers only recent seasons.Capture one book per season from 2012 to 2026 and record whether a Playtime Percentage block exists. Report the earliest season that carries it.open
2Does the playtime table appear in pre-season and post-season books?Week 1 depth is uncertain; pre-season snaps are the only official evidence available before it. Post-season books would extend the corpus.Capture one pre-season and one post-season book; record which blocks they carry.open
3Is a book published for a game that is postponed or cancelled?A missing book must be distinguishable from a capture failure, or the register will silently lose games.Check the 2026 schedule for any postponed game and query its game-centre page.open
4How quickly after a game's final whistle is the book published?This sets the earliest time the ground truth can be checked against a pre-game model.Poll one game-centre page every 15 minutes after kickoff and log the first minute the PDF URL appears.open
5Do the snap percentages always sum to 100 within a unit?If they do not, the table cannot be used as a share without a correction, and any correction would be an inference.Sum off_pct per club-game across the week already captured and publish the result.open
6Which slot labels vary between clubs, and does the parser cover them all?Cincinnati prints LDE/RDE/LOT/ROT/HB where most clubs print DE/T/RB. An unrecognised label loses that starter.Extract the distinct slot tokens across all 32 clubs and diff against the parser's vocabulary.open
7Does the Not Active list ever exceed the roster limit, or appear empty legitimately?An empty list could mean nobody was inactive or the block failed to parse; the two must not look the same.Count Not Active per club-game across a season and publish the distribution.open
8Can a player appear in both a club's Lineups block and its Not Active list?It should be impossible; if it happens the two blocks are being read from different pages.Left-join starters against inactives on game + club + player and report any hit.open
