We originated the side, the total and the moneyline — and the model lost to a spreadsheet

Two estimators were run over every completed regular-season game from 1999 to Week 10 of 2026, with a fixed chronological split decided in the source file before the first run. The opponent-adjusted model did not beat the unadjusted control on any market. Both lose to the benchmark on the side and the total. On the moneyline the control holds a small positive edge and the adjusted model does not. Rather than bury that, the whole project is published around it: the price store, the probability with its inputs, the proper score with its interval, and a named gate on every requirement that cannot yet be met.

The removal-of-luck panel

A staking run cannot be judged on its return at the sample sizes this project can reach. At the standard −110 price a bettor needs to win 52.38% just to break even, and separating a genuinely skilled bettor from a coin takes:

True win rateBets to separate it from a 50/50 coinBets to prove it is profitable at minus 110Method
55%6172243engine/linelab/scoring.py
57%314719engine/linelab/scoring.py
60%153263engine/linelab/scoring.py
52.38% (break-even itself)2725—engine/linelab/scoring.py

5% one-sided significance, 80% power, assuming independent bets. The two number columns answer different questions, and the difference matters: proving a 55% bettor is better than a coin takes 617 bets, but proving he is profitable at a minus-110 price takes 2243 — because the null he must clear is not 50% but the 52.38% break-even. Publishing only the easier number would make an edge look detectable long before it is bankable. Parlays need more still, because their outcomes cluster.

Read this before trusting any number here

The adjusted model loses. On the holdout, Brier skill versus a train-window base rate is +0.011 for the adjusted model against +0.069 for the unadjusted control on the moneyline, and both are negative on side and total. A more sophisticated model is not a better model, and this repository will not present it as one.
Most requirements are not met. 15 of 68 are blocked on a specific, named gate — most often the absence of a scheduled capture, an official inactive list, or depth coverage for all 32 clubs. The decision-readiness table names the gate and the number that would clear it for each.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
