Price store

Opening and closing numbers for every market we originate, with the basis of each number stated explicitly. The price-data policy was decided before any backtest was published, and it is the reason this page is mostly about what does not exist.

The policy, in four rules

1. Four line families, never merged. games.spread_line is a single undated line — labelled unspecified_single_line and excluded from any open-to-close computation. sc_lines.csv is a two-sided spread whose date is unstated — a fallback, never an opening price. initial_lines.csv is a genuine opening price and covers 2021 only. closing_lines.csv is a genuine closing price.
2. There is no opening moneyline. Anywhere. Not for 2021, not for any season. Moneyline is close-only, and its opening column is null on every row. This is published as a fact about the data rather than worked around by inference.
3. Only 272 games have both an opening and a closing side price — all of them in 2021. Measuring our line's movement from open to close is therefore not possible for current games until a daily price capture exists.
4. No price is ever inferred. A market without a dated opening price stores null. A closing price is never used as a stand-in for an opening one.

Coverage by season

Each row counts games in that season by the price evidence available. "Single line only" means the game has one undated number and nothing else.

How this was obtained, and why it matters. The nflverse release assets are the canonical long-run location, but they are served from a host this sandbox cannot reach. The same publisher's repository copies were used instead, via a repository tarball, and their hashes are on this page. The repository's existing GitHub Action runs where egress is open and can re-derive these from the release — which is the correct long-run fix.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
