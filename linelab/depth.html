Why "column 1 is the starter" is wrong on this data

25 slots list more than one player at rank 1. Clubs publish groups — three receivers, two cornerbacks — and choosing one of them as "the starter" would be an invention the club never made. Every such slot is stored as a group. 300 slots are published empty and are kept as rows rather than skipped, because "nobody listed" is information, not a gap.

The feature this section deliberately does not build

Backup deficiency: not built. The brief requires resolving the depth-order source before building this feature. Two routes were required to be evaluated in order: (a) per-club official pages, verified club by club; (b) snap counts from an official source. Route (a) is unavailable as an OFFICIAL source because no league-official chart exists, and only 5 of 32 clubs have been acquired. Route (b) has no official source reachable from this repository. The feature is therefore not built.

What would unblock it

  • acquisition and dated capture of all 32 club charts, or
  • an official snap-count or participation source for 2026
Shortcut refused: Deriving depth order from jersey number, experience, salary or roster order. None of those is a depth order and using them would fabricate a starter.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
