Availability

The official injury report is treated as a timestamped series. Every read is stored with its clock time and a SHA-256 of the bytes read, and the site publishes the change between two reads rather than a single level presented as stable. Absence from the report is never rendered as "available".

Capture defect detected mechanically: team_block_without_any_designation in 2026-11-14/week10-injury-report — team(s) BAL, 8 rows. A team whose entire block carries no game status and no non-participation practice flag is indistinguishable from 'everyone practised fully'. Any model that reads a blank cell as healthy will under-estimate that team's unavailability by exactly this many players.

Two possible causes, not yet distinguished: the league had not yet published game statuses for that team when the capture was taken / the capture omitted the column values for that block.
How to resolve: Take a third capture of the same week. If the block is populated, the first capture was a reading artefact; if it is still blank, the league had not published. Held open as an irregularity until that capture exists.

What "Questionable" actually means

Rather than quoting an old percentage, the designation is joined to observed participation across every season present in both files. A player counts as having played when a snap-participation row exists for his team-week. This is a participation join: it proves he was on the field, not that he started.

Out and Doubtful are near-certain absences, which is the useful finding: a Doubtful player essentially never plays. Questionable is close to a coin flip, which means it carries almost no information on its own and must be combined with practice participation to be a signal.

What this section cannot yet do. The brief asks for at least daily capture of the official report, club depth pages and inactive evidence. This repository holds 2 capture dates and cannot self-schedule. The cadence is the gap, not the format: a scheduled job that fetches, hashes and commits daily closes the scheduling requirements (R18, R28, R45, R52, R61) at once.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
