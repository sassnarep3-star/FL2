Promotion and demotion

Demotion gate. A live strategy returns to paper when the game-cluster lower bound of its Brier skill falls to zero or below over its last 100 prospective bets, or when its closing-line value is negative at the 95% level. Demotion is automatic and would be published the same day. Nothing has been promoted, so the gate currently has nothing to act on — but it exists so that promotion has a defined reversal.
Parlays and prop combos stay behind a joint-evidence gate. Multiplying marginal probabilities is not a joint probability. A combination may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability exists, and for prop combos the operator's void and push rules are also required. Neither exists here, so both divisions are registered as blocked rather than omitted.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
