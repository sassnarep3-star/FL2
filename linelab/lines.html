Our lines

Every model output is stored as a probability with the inputs that produced it, and the three columns the brief requires — fair line, market line, difference — are separate. The order is the brief's: side first, then the total, then the moneyline.

How a line is produced

The adjusted estimator fits, by ridge least squares, a baseline c, a home-field term h, an offence rating a[team] and a defence rating b[team] over every game that kicked off before this one:

  • home points = c + h + a[home] − b[away]
  • away points = c + a[away] − b[home]

The fair side line is the negative of the predicted home margin. The fair total is the predicted combined score. The cover and over probabilities come from a Normal with σ equal to the out-of-sample residual standard deviation of the last two seasons, never the in-sample one. The unadjusted control uses this season's mean points for and against, with no opponent adjustment.
