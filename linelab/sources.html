How to check any number here

  1. Open the artefact named on the decision-readiness row.
  2. Re-run the builder that produced it. Every builder is deterministic and re-runnable: price_store.py, model.py, scoring.py, availability.py, depth_graph.py, backtest.py, requirements.py.
  3. Compare the SHA-256 of any input against the value on Price store or Availability.
What is reused read-only and cannot be re-derived here. The 43 files in signal/data/ were produced by a workflow whose raw inputs were never committed. This site uses three of them for the designation-reliability join and labels them as such. They are listed on Flagged.

Not betting advice. This is a measurement project. Every value is either reproducible from a committed file in this repository or left blank; blanks are listed on Flagged. Model prices come from nflverse/nfldata, an open mirror of published official results and market numbers; availability comes from nfl.com.
