A live sign bug, found and fixed. The schedules file's spread_line is signed from the away team; read as a home line it shows a 56.93% home cover rate over 3,032 games. Corrected: 48.53% home cover, 49.00% over, and 98.92% agreement with the closing file's own scored outcome. Retire the naive reading. nflverse/nfldata ↗
An injury designation now has a measured value. Policy attaches no number to Doubtful or Questionable. Questionable played 65.68%, Doubtful 0.89%, Out 0.03%. Price availability from measurement, not opinion. NFL injury report ↗
The published depth chart is a weak replacement map. 1st string appears 90.62% vs 80.39% for 2nd string. Do not claim a "next man up" effect from the chart alone. depth charts ↗
Same-game correlation is near zero overall, real at big spreads. phi 0.013 overall; 0.058 at spreads of 7+. A big-favourite-cover plus over parlay is mispriced by the independence assumption. closing lines ↗
Six strategies tested, none survived. 0 of 6 cleared the pre-registered gate; best in-sample +3.75% with a CI spanning zero. Do not fund a leaderboard or stake plan yet. closing lines ↗
