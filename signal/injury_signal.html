Injury signal

What an official injury designation is actually worth, measured rather than assumed.

This replaces an assumption with a measurement. League policy attaches no numeric probability to Doubtful or Questionable, so any weighting was invented. We measured the realised participation rate instead.

A trap that produces a false answer

Joining official status only to the snap counts returns a 100% play rate for Questionable. That is survivorship bias, not evidence: the snap-count file lists only players who took a snap, so anyone who sat out has no row and silently leaves the denominator. The denominator used here is every player-week the official report lists, and the 100% figure is printed above only so it can be rejected.

Caveats carried with this number

  • Name-based joining can miss players renamed or traded mid-season; the unmatched share is reported below.
  • A player placed on injured reserve before the game may not appear in the report at all, so this measures listed players only.
  • This measures participation, not effectiveness. It says nothing about performance drop-off.
