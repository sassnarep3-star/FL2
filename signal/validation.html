Validation lab

Pre-registered gates, every strategy published, and zero survivors.

No strategy has cleared the gate. That is the result, not a failure of the page. Six pre-registered team-bet strategies were evaluated against a fixed gate. Zero cleared it. All six are published with the gate each one failed, because deleting the failures is the easiest way to manufacture apparent skill.

Caveats

  • Default -110 pricing is used where the schedule file carries no side price; a real account would face different prices and limits.
  • No stake sizing, bankroll rule or line-shopping is modelled.
  • The holdout seasons are the most recent three, but they were also used to build the descriptive pages, so they are not pristine. This is stated rather than hidden.
  • A strategy that fails G2 may still be real but underpowered; failing the gate means 'not yet demonstrated', not 'proven false'.
