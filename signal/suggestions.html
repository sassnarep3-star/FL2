Overview / Suggestions

Suggestions

A decision queue you can review before any change is implemented.

How to use this page. Each card is a proposal with the evidence we have, the evidence we lack, the cost, the benefit, a recommended decision, and the alternatives that were rejected. Set a decision to approved, rejected or deferred in data/suggestions.json. Nothing is implemented until it is approved. Items marked blocked cannot start until the linked questions are answered.

16 suggestions · 14 approved · 2 blocked by open questions. All items are published with a source link per row.

Every row links to the machine-readable register, which is the source of truth.

S03
Validation		Make the validation gate a hard, pre-registered, code-enforced gate
A leaderboard without a gate rewards whatever was tried last. Multiple comparisons guarantee some strategy looks good by luck.		A strategy cannot appear on the leaderboard unless it passes, in code: (1) a pre-registered minimum bet count; (2) a bootstrap CI on ROI whose lower bound exceeds zero after the vig; (3) a false-discovery-rate correction across all strategies attempted; (4) an untouched holdout period. Failures are listed, not deleted.P0 / cost medium / benefit highreadyQ03, Q04approvedNIST on CIs ↗

Validation		Split every published result into 'in-sample' and 'holdout' columns instead of one number
A single reported ROI hides whether the strategy was tuned on the same data it is scored on.		Two columns per strategy: in-sample and holdout, with the number of bets in each. A strategy with a great in-sample and negative holdout number is visibly fragile rather than hidden.P1 / cost low / benefit highreadyQ04approvedNIST CV methods ↗

Parlay pricing that assumes independence is silently wrong whenever legs correlate. The request explicitly asks for correlation values. S11
Signal design		Test every signal as a residual against the closing line, not against the raw outcome
Most apparent edges are re-discoveries of information already in the price. Scoring against the raw outcome cannot distinguish the two.		For each candidate signal, report two numbers: how well it predicts the outcome, and how well it predicts the ERROR of the closing line. Only the second indicates information the market had not priced.P0 / cost low / benefit highreadyQ12, Q08approvedclosing lines ↗
S12
Signal design		Use rest days, travel and short-week flags as candidate signals, and let the gate reject them
These are classic situational angles with weak evidence. They are cheap to test and easy to fool yourself with.		Compute rest differential and short-week flags per game, test against the close residual, and publish the result even if it is null. Publish the number of tests tried to keep the multiple-comparisons problem visible.P2 / cost low / benefit mediumreadyQ03, Q12proposedrest-day definition ↗
