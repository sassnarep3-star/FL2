Why some sections say "awaiting runner"

The development sandbox can reach github.com, codeload.github.com, api.github.com and pypi.org. It cannot reach release-assets.githubusercontent.com, where nflverse release assets are served, and it cannot reach www.nfl.com with curl. Bulk downloads therefore run in .github/workflows/fetch-nflverse-data.yml on a GitHub Actions runner, which has open egress. The closing-line and schedule files are small enough to arrive through codeload and are already verified.
