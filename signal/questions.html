Q05
Correlation		What are the empirically measured correlations between legs we intend to combine, and do they hold out of sample?
Parlay pricing assumes independence. If legs are positively correlated the true joint probability is higher than the product; the book prices a multiplied edge and we must know the sign and size.		· Play-by-play and player-week data allow us to compute empirical joint frequencies of outcomes within the same game. [S04]
· Games are identifiable by a stable game_id, so same-game legs can be joined reliably. [S08]
· MEASURED 2026-11-14: same-game correlation between the two team legs (home cover and over) is small. Over 2,927 graded games the joint probability is 24.09% versus 23.75% if independent, a difference of +0.33 percentage points (phi = 0.013). [S08]
· The correlation is concentrated in large spreads: phi = 0.058 for spreads of 7 or more (joint +1.44 points above independent), versus phi = -0.011 for spreads of 3 or less. [S08]		· Player-prop leg correlations. These need prop prices to value, which we do not hold (Q02).

Q06
Injury / availability		Does an official game status actually predict whether a player plays, and by how much, by position and by injury type?
The injury signal is the core of the availability model. If 'Questionable' has a stable play rate, it is a usable probability. If it does not, any weighting is invented.		· Official definitions: Out = will not play; Doubtful = unlikely to participate; Questionable = uncertain whether the player will play. [S02]
· Probable was removed because about 95% of Probable players did play. [S02]
· The policy sets no numeric probability for Doubtful or Questionable. [S02]
· MEASURED 2026-11-14: over 2016-2026, listed Questionable players actually participated 65.68% of the time (n=14,948; 95% CI 64.92-66.44%). Listed Doubtful participated 0.89% (n=1,684). Listed Out participated 0.03% (n=10,674). [S04]
· The Questionable rate is stable across 11 measured seasons (62.5% to 72.7%) and varies by position group: QB 42.3%, DB 58.0%, OL 58.2%, SKILL 61.7%, LB 65.1%, DL 66.2%. [S04]		· Whether the play rate holds within a single injury type (knee vs ankle vs illness).
· Whether 'Questionable' plus a full practice differs from 'Questionable' plus DNP.
· Whether the rate is stable enough by position to price an individual player.		Join official game status to actual participation (snap counts or player-week stats with attempts > 0) and compute realised play rates with Wilson intervals. This converts an assumption into a measurement.partially_answered

Q12
